SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

OCTOBER CLOSES AT 13,793u / $349,520

Final October totals: retail 13,233u / $335,520 across all 5 FF stores, and wholesale 560u / $14,000 from two new True Bliss locations (Duluth and Kennesaw). Wk4 retail delivered 2,685u / $62,734 on 10/26–10/27: FFD Athens 1,255u / $29,641, Decatur 499u / $11,773, Smyrna 400u / $9,100, Evans 360u / $8,300, and Macon 171u / $3,920. FFD Macon's opening order (4,528u / $118,945) was the largest single order of the month.

OCTOBER VS. SEPTEMBER: COMBINED REVENUE DOWN 7% ON ONE FEWER WEEK

Versus the September Final tab (15,180u / $374,581 over 5 delivery weeks), October's 4 weeks came in at 13,793u / $349,520: units down 9% and revenue down 7%. Retail revenue was down 3% ($335,520 vs. $345,581) even with FFD Macon's opening order, as AIO, Tinctures, Badder, and the R&D flower sold out at cultivation. Wholesale revenue was down 52% ($14,000 vs. $29,000), with no orders until the last week of the month.

TRUE BLISS ADDS TWO LOCATIONS: DULUTH AND KENNESAW

True Bliss – Duluth (Fine_F-118, 160u / $4,000, delivered 10/26) and True Bliss – Kennesaw (Fine_F-124, 400u / $10,000, delivering 10/30) both placed first orders off the expanded three-strain menu, each ordering Tucked In and Georgia Pie only. Kennesaw's opening order is 2.5x the size of Duluth's. True Bliss now has four locations under the TheraTrue buyer group in APEX: Buckhead, Downtown, Duluth, and Kennesaw.

PAYMENTS: $14,000 OPEN, ALL WITH TRUE BLISS

True Bliss – Duluth Fine_F-118 ($4,000) is due 11/9, and True Bliss – Kennesaw Fine_F-124 ($10,000) is due 11/13. Every other GA wholesale invoice is Fully Paid. True Bliss has paid between on time and 14 days late across six invoices; courtesy reminders around 11/6 and 11/10 would help keep both new locations on Net 14.

AIO, BADDER, AND R&D FLOWER SOLD OUT; NO NEW BATCHES SINCE 10/21

The last week of October cleared the remaining AIO, SAUS Badder, Uplift Soft Drops, and every R&D flower batch. After Kennesaw, cultivation holds about 5,881u, down from 12,902u on 10/2: flower 5,056u, Pineapple Express vapes 348u, Capsules 330u, Comfort Soft Drops 144u, and End Game Sugar Wax 3u. AIO and Tinctures are at 0u. With five stores drawing, new production is now the main constraint on retail restocks and any wholesale expansion.

WHOLESALE MENU GOING INTO NOVEMBER: ABOUT 55 CASES, GEORGIA PIE DOWN TO 5

The wholesale menu has Tucked In 1,670u (41 cases), OG Kush batch 1 378u (9 cases), and Georgia Pie batch 2 200u (5 cases), plus 1,137u on deck. Both new True Bliss locations ordered Georgia Pie, so batch 2 may run out on the next reorder while Georgia Pie batch 1 (834u) stays restricted for retail.

SOFT DROPS AND CAPSULES: 40u CASES AND WHOLESALE PRICING SET IN APEX

All gummies and capsules are now set at 40 units per case, with wholesale priced at 50% of menu retail. 200mg Soft Drops: $38 retail, $19 wholesale, $760 per case. Capsules: 100mg $30 retail / $15 wholesale ($600 per case), 200mg $40 / $20 ($800 per case), and 400mg $50 / $25 ($1,000 per case). To compete once these launch to wholesale, the proposed retail price points are 100mg $25, 200mg $35, and 400mg $45, which at 50% would put wholesale at $12.50, $17.50, and $22.50 ($500, $700, and $900 per case).

SAUS END GAME SUGAR WAX AND COMFORT SOFT DROPS SELL THROUGH IN A WEEK

Both new items packaged 10/21 went out to all 5 stores within a week: End Game Sugar Wax 232 of 235u at $30/unit and Comfort Soft Drops 320 of 464u at $20/unit. The 144u of Comfort left is the only Soft Drops supply at cultivation.

CARRIED FROM SEPTEMBER: MENU EXPANSION, CASE SIZING, PIPELINE

Cultivation team confirmed case counts moving forward: Concentrates and 1,200mg Tinctures will be packed at 20 units per case (larger jars), and all other products at 40 units per case. Limited biomass availability will have us holding off from launching categories with favorable wholesale margins like capsules and soft drops, until we have enough distillate and production capabilities to keep a consistent demand for our stores with slight excess to offer for wholesale accounts.

EDIBLE BATCH INCONSISTENCIES: MG VARIANCE WELL OUTSIDE A ±5% STANDARD

Recent gummy, capsule, and tincture batches are testing well off their labeled dose. Most 200mg batches come in 10% to 20% low, and some are much further off: two Dr. Henry's Soft Drops batches are short by 77mg to 88mg (38% to 44%). Our 1,200mg tincture is 127mg short, and one capsule batch tests 28% over label. The newest Comfort Soft Drops batch (10/21) also tests 13% low at 174.81mg. Only a few batches fall within ±5%. Recommendation: set a ±5% release spec and remediate any batch outside it before it ships, so patients get consistent, reliable dosing.
