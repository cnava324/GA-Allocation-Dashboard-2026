SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

WK2 (8/15) — NO DELIVERIES, RETAIL PRE-ORDERS HELD FOR VAPE BUNDLING

No wholesale or retail deliveries this week. Retail pre-orders (717u across End Game, OG Kush, Hotlanta, and Soft Drops) were intentionally held back so they can be delivered alongside the pending 0.5g Vape restock once test results return, rather than making two separate trips. Pending delivery 8/18–8/19.

RETAIL FLOWER SUPPLY HEALTHY — SURPLUS OPENED TO WHOLESALE

Per Hoodie Analytics, retail stores are projected to carry 24.8–52.5 days of 3.5g Flower supply. With that cushion, Hotlanta and Night Cap — both previously retail-exclusive — were opened up to wholesale this week alongside Jai Alai and Cakewalk, giving Chris more SKUs to offer wholesale and pharmacy accounts.

TRUE BLISS BUCKHEAD — NEW WHOLESALE ACCOUNT, FIRST PRE-ORDER

True Bliss – Buckhead (a new location) submitted a pre-order for 560u across Jai Alai, Cakewalk, Hotlanta, and Night Cap, pending delivery 8/19. True Bliss Downtown Atlanta has not yet placed an order this cycle.

BOTANICAL SCIENCES — NO RESPONSE DESPITE OUTREACH

Several outreach attempts to Botanical Sciences this week went unanswered, even after Fine Fettle submitted an order for their wholesale products. Chris's read is that they may be waiting to see GA's 0.5g Vape line before engaging further — worth revisiting once vape test results are back.

NIGHT CAP RETURNS, END GAME LAUNCHES

Night Cap (650u) is back in the mix after selling through in July. End Game (940u) launches as a brand-new, unrestricted flower SKU and already drew retail pre-order interest across all 4 stores this week.

AURA 1g DISPOSABLES SOLD OUT AGAIN — BACKUP BATCHES IN THE PRODUCTION PIPELINE

The first two AURA AIO batches out of testing (Galactic Gas 963u, Super Lemon Haze 931u) were delivered entirely to retail in Wk1, leaving 0 available at cultivation before any wholesale allocation could be made. Once we have a clear understanding of demand and strains in rotation, increasing batch size from 900 to 1,500–1,800 units per production run would build enough surplus to support a wholesale allocation alongside retail, and would reduce how often new production/testing cycles are needed. Worth tracking whether the same logic should extend to Vape (510) batch sizing once confirmed.

CAKEWALK JOINS THE WHOLESALE MENU — TWO-BATCH STRUCTURE (WK1)

Cakewalk carries two batches at cultivation (374u active + 1,059u on-deck) and was made available on the wholesale menu alongside Jai Alai in Wk1. The on-deck batch will automatically activate once the first sells through.

HOTLANTA & OG KUSH LAUNCHED RETAIL-EXCLUSIVE (WK1) — HOTLANTA NOW OPEN TO WHOLESALE

Hotlanta (Georgia Pie x Pave) and OG Kush launched retail-exclusive in Wk1. As of this week, Hotlanta has opened up to wholesale (see above); OG Kush remains the sole fully Restricted, retail-only flower SKU.

0.5g VAPES (510) — STILL IN TESTING, NO CHANGE

Vapes remain sold out at cultivation with no deliveries to either channel this week. Carrying forward the 2–3 week testing timeline flagged at the end of July.
