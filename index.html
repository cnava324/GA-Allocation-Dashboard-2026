SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

SEPTEMBER CLOSES AT 15,180u / $374,581 — RETAIL UP 84% VS. AUGUST

Final September totals: Wholesale 1,160u / $29,000 across 5 orders from 4 accounts (Bonaire x2, True Bliss – Buckhead, True Bliss – Downtown, The Pharmacy Place). Retail 14,020u / $345,581 across all 4 FF stores. Versus the August Final tab, wholesale revenue came in slightly lower ($29,000 vs. $31,500) while retail revenue rose 84% ($345,581 vs. $187,422), driven by the return of AIO, the Cherry Vanilla Tincture launch, and the new Tucked In / Night Cap flower batches. Wk5 (9/28–9/29) was the largest retail week of the month at 4,621u / $111,588.

Soft Drops (50u/case), Tinctures (121u/case), and Capsules (100u/case) use their own observed case sizes, consistent with prior months. THE PHARMACY PLACE — THIRD INDEPENDENT PHARMACY ACCOUNT, FIRST ORDER DELIVERED

The Pharmacy Place (Peachtree City) placed its first order on 9/28 and received it 9/29 (Fine_F-109): Tucked In 40u and Night Cap 80u, $3,000. This makes them Fine Fettle's third independent pharmacy account after Tifton Drug Company and Bonaire Pharmacy. They were previously noted as investing in a Sweed centralized POS before onboarding, so it is worth confirming whether that system is now live or whether they brought our product in ahead of it.

INDEPENDENT PHARMACY PIPELINE — REMAINING LEADS POS-GATED

Professional Pharmacy (Dalton) is awaiting our Dutchie POS rollout before bringing our product in. A new pharmacy location in Canton also reached out for an introductory call. The Pharmacy Place has now converted (see above). The remaining leads are gated on one POS decision or another, reinforcing the priority of solidifying the flow with Releaf POS (Bonaire Pharmacies POS system they created and catered for their business).

TIFTON DRUG COMPANY — NO SEPTEMBER REORDER

Tifton's only order to date was Fine_F-82 ($3,700, delivered 8/24, paid 9/9). They did not reorder in September and do not appear on this month's buyer performance report. Worth a check-in, especially now that high-potency Tucked In and the new Night Cap batch give us fresh flower to offer.

TRUE BLISS — BOTH ATLANTA LOCATIONS REORDER (WK4, 720u, $18,000)

True Bliss – Buckhead (Fine_F-103: Jai Alai 360u, Night Cap 40u; $10,000) and True Bliss – Downtown (Fine_F-104: Jai Alai 320u; $8,000) both placed and received orders on 9/23, ordered 9/21. FLOWER RESTOCK — TUCKED IN AND NEW NIGHT CAP BATCH LEAD THE MONTH'S LARGEST RETAIL WEEK

Two new high-potency flower batches arrived: Tucked In (21.3% THC, roughly 4,030u in the batch) and a new Night Cap batch (21.63% THC, roughly 1,719u). Together with OG Kush and End Game, they made their first retail allocations this week (Tucked In 1,640u, Night Cap 960u, OG Kush 560u, End Game 480u). Under the retail-first release policy, all four are now eligible to open to wholesale, and an 800u Tucked In line is already unrestricted in the export. This reopens meaningful wholesale flower supply after last week's shortage: roughly 1,950u of Tucked In and 559u of new Night Cap remain unreserved.
