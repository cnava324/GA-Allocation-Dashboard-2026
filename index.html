SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

AURA 1g DISPOSABLES SOLD OUT AGAIN — RECOMMEND RAISING BATCH SIZE

The first two AURA AIO batches out of testing (Galactic Gas 963u, Super Lemon Haze 931u) were delivered entirely to retail this week, leaving 0 available at cultivation before any wholesale allocation could be made. Per Chris's recommendation, increasing batch size from 900 to 1,500–1,800 units per production run would build enough surplus to support a wholesale allocation alongside retail, and would reduce how often new production/testing cycles are needed. Worth tracking whether the same logic should extend to Vape (510) batch sizing once that line clears testing.

CAKEWALK JOINS THE WHOLESALE MENU — TWO-BATCH STRUCTURE

Cakewalk now carries two batches at cultivation (374u active + 1,059u on-deck) and has been made available on the wholesale menu alongside Jai Alai. The on-deck batch will automatically activate once the first sells through, giving Chris a second flower SKU to pitch to wholesale accounts and independent pharmacies — useful timing given the outreach push already underway.

TWO NEW RETAIL-EXCLUSIVE FLOWER SKUS — HOTLANTA & OG KUSH

Hotlanta (Georgia Pie x Pave) and OG Kush launched this week and are both flagged Restricted (retail-only). Combined with existing retail-exclusive Keepsake and Open Mic, retail now carries four flower SKUs beyond the two wholesale-eligible ones (Jai Alai, Cakewalk).

0.5g VAPES (510) — STILL IN TESTING, NO CHANGE

Vapes remain sold out at cultivation with no deliveries to either channel this week. Carrying forward the 2–3 week testing timeline flagged at the end of July.

WK1 (8/8) — RETAIL ONLY, NO WHOLESALE ORDERS

The first week of August had no wholesale deliveries. Section 1 should start populating once Chris's independent pharmacy outreach along the delivery routes — using Jai Alai, and now Cakewalk — begins converting to orders.

JAI ALAI UNTOUCHED THIS WEEK — WATCH FOR WHOLESALE PRIORITIZATION

Jai Alai (2,668u available) wasn't drawn on by retail this week, unlike every other flower SKU. With retail-exclusive strains selling through quickly, this may be a good window to prioritize Jai Alai toward wholesale/pharmacy outreach before retail demand shifts back to it.

NO DEMAND PLAN — ALLOCATION-ONLY FOR NOW

This tracker currently shows actual units and revenue delivered only, with no attainment percentage or target comparison, since GA has no demand plan yet.
