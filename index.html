SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

BONAIRE PHARMACY — SECOND INDEPENDENT PHARMACY DELIVERY COMPLETE

Bonaire Pharmacy's (Warner Robins) first order (40u each Jai Alai, Cakewalk, Night Cap; $3,000) delivered 9/2 — Fine Fettle's second completed independent pharmacy delivery after Tifton Drug Company. Outreach to pharmacies will continue to gauge which pharmacy partners would be best to start wholesale relationships with. We're also stalling adding more pharmacy accounts unless the retailer has a centralized POS system or is open to adding our products into their internal pharmacy POS system. Onboarding is being stalled with vendors until we can solidify a streamlined Dutchie POS operational flow with retailers who prefer operating with multiple POS systems.

HOTLANTA SOLD OUT — FIRST STRAIN TO FULLY DEPLETE

Hotlanta's last 10u were delivered to FFD Athens this week, and the strain no longer appears in the cultivation inventory export. It is the first flower strain to fully sell through since tracking began — worth watching whether a new batch is in the production pipeline.

0.5g VAPES — FULL RESTOCK DELIVERED, SUPPLY TIGHT AGAIN

This week's 1,920u retail delivery (Galactic Gas + Lemon Cherry Gelato across all 4 FF stores) used nearly the entire supply that cleared testing last week. Only 13u remain at cultivation. No wholesale allocation has been made yet — recommend holding off on WS Vape outreach until a larger batch clears, consistent with last month's guidance.

DATA FLAG — RESERVED/ON ORDER SHOWING 0 ACROSS ALL CATEGORIES

This week's inventory export shows 0 reserved/on-order units for every category, including Flower, where prior snapshots carried meaningful reserved balances tied to pending pre-orders. Worth confirming with the Macon team whether this reflects an empty pre-order queue as of 9/4, or a gap in this week's export.

CAPSULES — LARGE INVENTORY INCREASE, WORTH CONFIRMING

Capsules availability jumped from 276u to 1,426u in this snapshot, driven by a new Doctor Henry's Comfort batch (531u) and FF Capsules 400mg 20:1 (619u). New batches will be allocated towards our retail stores on their future deliveries.

TIFTON DRUG COMPANY — DUTCHIE POS EXPLORATION ONGOING - JANE CONFIRMED SEAMLESS INTEGRATIONS ONCE DUTCHIE IS SOLIDIFIED

No new update this week; Chris remains in touch with Dutchie about a third POS option for Tifton to run alongside the pharmacy's existing systems, per last month's notes. Dutchie confirmed a 3–4 week lead time on this to be complete.
