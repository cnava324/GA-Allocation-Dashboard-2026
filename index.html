CASE SIZING — PROPOSED 10 / 20 / 30 TIER STRUCTURE FOR WHOLESALE

Current tracker assumptions: Flower, Vapes, and AIO at 40u/case; Soft Drops 50u/case; Tinctures 121u/case; Capsules 100u/case. Before new categories launch to wholesale, case counts need to be realistic for buyers based on wholesale cost per unit, retail velocity/sell-through, and product unit size. The goal is to put higher-priced or larger items in cases of 10–20 units, and smaller or faster-selling items in cases of 30 (as in CT) or the current GA 40. Recommendation: standardize on 10 / 20 / 30 depending on the product and keep that consistent going forward. For reference, Botanical Sciences offers 10-unit cases for its higher-priced live resin and live rosin vapes, and other GA competitors commonly offer 10-unit cases.

  • Suggested starting point for discussion (using September average unit prices): FF 1,200mg Tinctures (~$45/u) at 10u/case (~$446/case); Dr. Henry's 200mg Tinctures (~$25/u) and Capsules (~$20/u) at 20u/case (~$400–$500/case); 1g AIO (~$37.50/u) at 20u/case (~$750/case); Soft Drops (~$21/u), 0.5g Vapes ($20/u), and 3.5g Flower (~$24–25/u) at 30u/case (~$600–$750/case).
  • This would bring the largest single-case commitment down from $1,000 (40u of flower) to roughly $750, and the smallest to under $450, which should make first orders easier for independent pharmacies.
