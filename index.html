SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

FFD MACON OPENS 10/15, OPENING ORDER DELIVERED: 4,528u / $118,945

FFD Macon received its opening order (Fine_F-102) on Wed 10/14, in time for its Thu 10/15 opening, making it Fine Fettle's fifth GA store. The order covered all seven retail categories: Flower 2,360u, AIO 880u, Vapes 320u, Capsules 300u, Soft Drops 284u, Tinctures 264u, and Concentrates 120u. It came in 480u larger than the 4,048u reserved, adding the new R&D strains, Bright Side, Velvet Punch, and SAUS Badder. The invoice now shows $118,944.82, up from the $67,876.82 on file. With it delivered, nothing at cultivation is reserved.

WHOLESALE FLOWER MENU CONFIRMED: THREE STRAINS, ~79 CASES ACTIVE

Georgia Pie batch 2 (680u, 19.18%), OG Kush batch 1 (578u, 12.17%), and Tucked In batch 1 (1,950u, 21.3%) are on the public wholesale menu, about 3,208u. Two more batches are on deck: Tucked In batch 2 (718u, 17.58%, new this week) and OG Kush batch 2 (419u, 16.42%), for 4,345u once they activate. That is up from one strain and 20 cases at the start of October. Retail can still draw on these lines: Smyrna took 160u of OG Kush batch 1 this week, dropping its wholesale quantity from 738u to 578u.

OCTOBER WK2, RETAIL-ONLY WEEK: 7,413u / $187,282, THE LARGEST WEEK TRACKED

No wholesale orders this week. Retail delivered to all 5 FF stores: FFD Macon 4,528u / $118,945 (10/14), Athens 965u / $23,038 (10/15), Decatur 840u / $20,400 (10/14), Smyrna 780u / $18,000 (10/14), and Evans 300u / $6,900 (10/15). Without the Macon opening order, the four existing stores took 2,885u / $68,338, mostly flower and the new Concentrates. October retail MTD is 10,548u / $272,786. Athens and Evans (Fine_F-114, 117) still show In-transit in the export despite their 10/15 delivery date.

R&D FLOWER BATCHES CLEARED TESTING AND MOSTLY SOLD THROUGH

The R&D batches that passed testing late last week went straight into this week's store orders. Hulkberry (360 of 414u), Jai Alai x Finessin #2 (320 of 364u), and Jai Alai x Finessin #1 (360 of 486u, 25.78% THC, listed as Eazy Peazy in last week's export) are down to one to three cases each. A new R&D strain, Fritterz x Finessin (20.06%, 527u), went out at 160u with 367u left, and Velvet Punch is down to 62u. All remain restricted for the FF stores until in-store stock is high enough to open more strains to wholesale.

NEW CATEGORY: SAUS 1G CONCENTRATES (BADDER)

Three small SAUS Badder batches were added this week: Georgia Pie (66.12%), OG Kush (76.24%), and Velvet Punch (73.32%), 598u in total. 580u went to all 5 stores at $30/unit in 20u cases ($600/case), leaving 18u, all below a full case. Concentrates are added as a new row in Sections 2–4 with a 20u case size. That case size already fits the proposed 10 / 20 / 30 structure, so Concentrates could be a clean wholesale test once batch sizes grow.

PAYMENTS: TRUE BLISS $18,000 NOW 8 DAYS PAST DUE

True Bliss – Buckhead Fine_F-103 ($10,000) and True Bliss – Downtown Fine_F-104 ($8,000), due 10/7, still show Unpaid as of 10/15. Their slowest prior payment was Downtown's July invoice (Fine_F-52), paid 14 days late. Every other GA wholesale invoice is Fully Paid. With three strains now open on the wholesale menu, settling these invoices before shipping any new True Bliss order would keep the open balance from growing.

AURA PINEAPPLE EXPRESS 510 VAPES RETURN (948u)

A new Pineapple Express 0.5g batch (88.34%, 948u) was added this week, restricted for the FF stores. None went out this week; it will be allocated to the stores next week. The older Green Crack and Pineapple Express batches sold through to FFD Macon.

AIO, TINCTURES, SOFT DROPS, CAPSULES: SUPPLY THIN AFTER MACON

AIO is down to 267u (Lemon Cherry Gelato 147u, King Louis XIII 120u) and Green Crack is sold out. Tinctures are sold out at cultivation. Soft Drops are down to 97u of Uplift, and Capsules hold at 330u (Sleep 199u, Comfort 131u). With five stores now drawing, these categories need new production to cover retail before any wholesale test, which makes the August recommendation for larger AIO batches (1,500–1,800u) more pressing.

CARRIED FROM SEPTEMBER: MENU EXPANSION, CASE SIZING, PIPELINE

Wholesale menu expansion beyond flower and vapes remains open; the margin hurdle is cleared for Capsules, Soft Drops, and select Tinctures, and the 10 / 20 / 30 case-size proposal is still pending a decision. Professional Pharmacy (Dalton) and the Canton lead remain POS-gated. Tifton is being onboarded to Releaf POS by the end of October, with the GA Map Locator in development to raise comffy brand awareness.
