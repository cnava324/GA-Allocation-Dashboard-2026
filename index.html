Suggestion: get expected release dates from production for the next vape, AIO, edible, and tincture batches so Bridget can plan store restocks and we can tell wholesale accounts what's coming.

Retail took 960u of Tucked In batch 1 this week, cutting it from 1,670u to 710u (17 cases). Georgia Pie batch 2 is sold out (Kennesaw 200u, FFD Athens 200u); from batch 1, about 10 cases (434u) are held for the FF stores and 2 cases (80u) are open to wholesale so accounts like Bonaire can try it. All three of True Bliss's newest orders included Georgia Pie, so with only 2 cases open, True Bliss reorders will likely shift to Tucked In or OG Kush.

SOFT DROPS AND CAPSULES: 40u CASES AND WHOLESALE PRICING SET IN APEX

All gummies and capsules are now set at 40 units per case, with wholesale priced at 50% of menu retail. 200mg Soft Drops: $38 retail, $19 wholesale, $760 per case. Capsules: 100mg $30 retail / $15 wholesale ($600 per case), 200mg $40 / $20 ($800 per case), and 400mg $50 / $25 ($1,000 per case). To compete once these launch to wholesale, the proposed retail price points are 100mg $25, 200mg $35, and 400mg $45, which at 50% would put wholesale at $12.50, $17.50, and $22.50 ($500, $700, and $900 per case).

CARRIED FROM OCTOBER: MENU EXPANSION, CASE SIZING, PIPELINE

Cultivation team confirmed case counts moving forward: Concentrates and 1,200mg Tinctures will be packed at 20 units per case (larger jars), and all other products at 40 units per case. Limited biomass availability will have us holding off from launching categories with favorable wholesale margins like capsules and soft drops, until we have enough distillate and production capabilities to keep a consistent demand for our stores with slight excess to offer for wholesale accounts.

EDIBLE BATCH INCONSISTENCIES: MG VARIANCE WELL OUTSIDE A ±5% STANDARD

Recent gummy, capsule, and tincture batches are testing well off their labeled dose. Most 200mg batches come in 10% to 20% low, and some are much further off: two Dr. Henry's Soft Drops batches are short by 77mg to 88mg (38% to 44%). Our 1,200mg tincture is 127mg short, and one capsule batch tests 28% over label. The 10/21 Comfort Soft Drops batch, now sold out, also tested 13% low at 174.81mg. Only a few batches fall within ±5%. Recommendation: set a ±5% release spec and remediate any batch outside it before it ships, so patients get consistent, reliable dosing.
