SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

VAPE PRODUCTION IN TESTING — 2-3 WEEK PROJECTED TIMELINE

Vapes have been effectively sold out at cultivation for several weeks. Per Chris, the first batches from the new production run were sent out for testing this week, with new Vape and Flower SKUs projected to become available over the next 2-3 weeks. Until then, expect Section 4 to keep showing 0 available for Vapes.

LIMITED FLOWER CASE COUNTS — RETAIL-EXCLUSIVE, JAI ALAI ONLY ON WS MENU

With flower supply currently limited while new batches clear testing, Chris confirmed the available case counts are being reserved exclusively for FF retail stores. Jai Alai is the only SKU currently visible/orderable on the wholesale menu for external accounts — this lines up with the inventory export, where Jai Alai is the sole unrestricted Flower SKU and Cakewalk/Keepsake/Open Mic are flagged Restricted.

WHOLESALE OUTREACH & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

Chris was able to reach out to wholesale accounts Monday afternoon with the remaining flower inventory available for WS sales. Worth noting: between Monday and Tuesday last week, True Bliss added 6 new flower SKUs and 6 variants of 1g vapes — sourced from Botanical Sciences. Botanical Sciences currently has a larger, more varied supply and can stock both their own locations, True Bliss, and independent pharmacies with more options than we can right now. The long-term play is to be first to consistently dial in quality and testing metrics so our flower brands earn trust on the COA — other brands may have more variety early, but repeat purchases will follow whoever proves out as consistently better quality. Chris will begin outreach to independent pharmacies spaced out along our delivery routes to gauge interest in our Jai Alai (S) flower while we await more flower variety and a consistent supply of 0.5g Vapes and 1g Disposables.

WK4 (7/25) — RETAIL ONLY, VAPES FULLY DISTRIBUTED

Week of 7/25's report was retail-only — no wholesale deliveries, confirmed ahead of time. The last of the Vape/AIO stock at Macon went to FFD Evans, so every FF store carried AURA vapes between that week's and the prior week's deliveries — vapes have since sold through completely (see testing/timeline note above).

1g DISPOSABLES (AIO) — FIRST SALES, PRICE ESTABLISHED

AIO had its first wholesale sales in Wk2: 42 units across 3 AURA SKUs at $37.50/unit ($1,575), establishing a real price in place of the $0.00 placeholder. Retail also delivered 960u of AIO in Wk1 at $37.50/unit — same price point across both channels. No AIO activity since Wk4; also sold out at cultivation.

THREE NEW RETAIL-ONLY CATEGORIES — RETAIL ONLY FOR NOW

Soft Drops, Tinctures, and Capsules are tracked as retail-only per direction — they're not wholesaled currently and Section 1 (WS) is unaffected. These could move to wholesale later if inventory and margins support it, but pricing is only established for Flower and Vapes right now, so any near-term wholesale expansion should stay limited to those two categories until Soft Drops/Tinctures/Capsules/AIO pricing is confirmed.

CASE SIZE ASSUMPTION — 40 UNITS/CASE (FLOWER, VAPES, AIO ONLY)

July Cases for the original 3 WS-scope categories continues to use the 40-unit case size established at launch. The 3 new retail categories each use their own observed case size instead (see Section 2 notes), since the underlying order data made those explicit — flag if any of these assumptions need correcting.

NO DEMAND PLAN — ALLOCATION-ONLY FOR NOW

This tracker currently shows actual units and revenue delivered only, with no attainment percentage or target comparison, since GA has no demand plan. Once a demand plan is built (targeted for August), this workbook can adopt the same H2 Forecast / Retail Demand / Weeks of Supply structure used for CT, including the Overstocked (>6 wks) / Understocked (<2 wks) signal in Section 4.
