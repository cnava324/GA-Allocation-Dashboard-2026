SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

0.5g VAPES (510) RETURN — FIRST NEW BATCHES CLEAR TESTING

Two new AURA batches (Galactic Gas 967u, Lemon Cherry Gelato 966u) cleared testing this week — the first Vape supply since the restock began. Test failures on all other pending batches mean these two are being kept exclusive to retail stores for now, to keep them stocked, rather than splitting supply toward wholesale. Once more passing batches come through the pipeline without failures, wholesale allocation can be revisited.

TIFTON DRUG COMPANY — FIRST INDEPENDENT PHARMACY DELIVERY COMPLETE

Tifton Drug Company's order (160u across Open Mic, Cakewalk, End Game; $3,700) delivered 8/24 — Fine Fettle's first completed independent pharmacy delivery. Per Chris, further pharmacy outreach beyond the accounts already in motion remains paused until an internal strategy for these accounts is finalized.

TIFTON DRUG COMPANY — EXPLORING DUTCHIE FOR A SEAMLESS POS FLOW

Chris has been in touch with Dutchie about a third POS system for Tifton to run alongside his existing setup. For now, Tifton has added Fine Fettle's products into his current pharmacy POS and isn't in a rush to consolidate, since his existing systems are supplied by other vendors at no cost to him. He's more interested in a seamless retail flow through Dutchie specifically, and is open to paying up to $100/month for a system to sell Fine Fettle's product.

TRUE BLISS DOWNTOWN — FIRST ORDER OF THE MONTH

True Bliss – Downtown placed and received its first order of the August cycle (560u across Jai Alai, Open Mic, Cakewalk, End Game, Night Cap; $13,800), delivered 8/26 — about a week after Buckhead submitted its own initial order. Downtown is stocking up heavily on flower, which could mean they're expecting shortages, seeing strong sell-through, or both — worth keeping an eye on how quickly they reorder.

BONAIRE PHARMACY — SECOND INDEPENDENT PHARMACY ACCOUNT ONBOARDING

Bonaire Pharmacy (Warner Robins, GA) has an approved pre-order for 120u across Jai Alai, Cakewalk, and Night Cap ($3,000), pending delivery 9/2 — the second independent pharmacy account in the pipeline alongside Tifton. Bonaire is fully aware of the Dutchie POS hold-up but wants to bring Fine Fettle's product into their existing pharmacy POS now rather than wait, while they build toward a centralized POS with ecommerce of their own — a different long-term approach than continuing to rely on multiple vendor-controlled systems.

RETAIL ORDERS QUEUED FOR NEXT WEEK — FLOWER, VAPES & SOFT DROPS

No retail deliveries this week, but a large bundle is approved and queued: Flower, the returning Vapes, and Soft Drops across FFD Athens, Decatur, Evans, and Smyrna, delivering 8/31–9/1.

SUB-CASE QUANTITIES PULLED FROM WHOLESALE MENU

Standing policy: any SKU with less than a full case (40u) available at cultivation is automatically removed from the public wholesale menu and reserved for Fine Fettle's own retail locations. This currently applies to Cakewalk's depleted first batch (14u) and Hotlanta (10u) — both are retail-only until their next batch clears.

CAKEWALK BATCH 1 NEARLY DEPLETED — BATCH 2 NOW PRIMARY SUPPLY

Cakewalk's first batch is down to just 14u (below a full case, so pulled from the wholesale menu per policy); the on-deck second batch (1,059u) has already activated and is now carrying the bulk of both wholesale and retail draw.

HOTLANTA SUPPLY TIGHTENING — STILL AT 10 UNITS

Hotlanta remains at 10u available with no further draw this week — below a full case, so it's off the wholesale menu per policy and retail-only until the next batch comes in.

BOTANICAL SCIENCES — VAPE OUTREACH ON HOLD, FLOWER OUTREACH CONTINUES

Botanical Sciences has been responsive to Fine Fettle's own order requests for their wholesale products, but has not responded to any outreach about carrying Fine Fettle's flower. They were most interested in the 0.5g Vapes, but with only a few units remaining per batch after fully allocating to Fine Fettle's own stores, that conversation is on hold until a larger batch clears. Outreach will continue to see if their stores can offer Fine Fettle's flower in the meantime.

AURA 1g DISPOSABLES SOLD OUT AGAIN — BACKUP BATCHES IN THE PRODUCTION PIPELINE

The first two AURA AIO batches out of testing (Galactic Gas 963u, Super Lemon Haze 931u) were delivered entirely to retail in Wk1, leaving 0 available at cultivation before any wholesale allocation could be made. Once we have a clear understanding of demand and strains in rotation, increasing batch size from 900 to 1,500–1,800 units per production run would build enough surplus to support a wholesale allocation alongside retail, and would reduce how often new production/testing cycles are needed.

0.5g VAPES — HOLD OFF ON BATCH SIZE INCREASES UNTIL FAILURES ARE DIAGNOSED

Unlike AIO, Vape batch size should not be increased right now. With multiple pending Vape batches failing testing, the priority is identifying the root cause and getting a few consistent passing results from smaller batch runs first — increasing batch size before that risks a larger, more costly failure. Revisit batch sizing only after production is running reliably.
