CARRIED FROM OCTOBER: MENU EXPANSION, CASE SIZING, PIPELINE

Cultivation team confirmed case counts moving forward: Concentrates and 1,200mg Tinctures will be packed at 20 units per case (larger jars), and all other products at 40 units per case. Limited biomass availability will have us holding off from launching categories with favorable wholesale margins like capsules and soft drops, until we have enough distillate and production capabilities to keep a consistent demand for our stores with slight excess to offer for wholesale accounts.

Raised the flag with our cultivation team that our case sizes of 40 units may be too much for the new GA market as we are getting complaints in CT about our 30u case counts as purchasers prefer 15-20 unit cases so it'll have a higher percentage of selling off the retail menus with more variety of options compared to less variety and deeper case counts that can tie up cash with product sitting on the shelf if it doesn't move more than 1 unit per day.

EDIBLE BATCH INCONSISTENCIES: MG VARIANCE WELL OUTSIDE A ±5% STANDARD

Recent gummy, capsule, and tincture batches are testing well off their labeled dose. Most 200mg batches come in 10% to 20% low, and some are much further off: two Dr. Henry's Soft Drops batches are short by 77mg to 88mg (38% to 44%). Our 1,200mg tincture is 127mg short, and one capsule batch tests 28% over label. Only a few batches fall within ±5%. Recommendation: set a ±5% release spec and remediate any batch outside it before it ships, so patients get consistent, reliable dosing. The 500mg Balm tests 6% under label (469.22mg), and the new Uplift capsules need a dose check (above).
