SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

TIFTON DRUG COMPANY — FIRST INDEPENDENT PHARMACY PRE-ORDER, PENDING CONFIRMATION

Tifton Drug Company (Tifton, GA) submitted a pre-order for 160u across Open Mic, Cakewalk, and End Game ($3,700), pending delivery 8/24 — Fine Fettle's first independent pharmacy wholesale account. Per Chris, this delivery is still awaiting internal confirmation before moving forward, and further independent pharmacy outreach is paused until an internal strategy for these accounts is finalized.

TRUE BLISS BUCKHEAD — FIRST WHOLESALE DELIVERY OF THE MONTH

True Bliss – Buckhead's Wk2 pre-order (560u across Jai Alai, Cakewalk, Hotlanta, Night Cap; $14,000) delivered this week — the first wholesale delivery of the August cycle. True Bliss Downtown Atlanta has still not placed an order.

HELD RETAIL ORDERS DELIVERED — BUNDLED WITH NEW FFD ATHENS ORDER

The Wk2 pre-orders held for vape bundling (End Game, OG Kush, Hotlanta, Soft Drops) delivered this week across all 4 stores, alongside a new order to FFD Athens (Hotlanta, Jai Alai). Vapes and AIO remain the only categories still pending test results.

HOTLANTA SUPPLY TIGHTENING — DOWN TO 10 UNITS

Hotlanta dropped to just 10u available at cultivation after this week's combined wholesale (240u) and retail (120u) draw. Worth watching closely — it may need to come off the wholesale menu temporarily if retail demand keeps pace.

OG KUSH FULLY CLEARED AT CULTIVATION

OG Kush sold through completely this week (37u delivered to FFD Decatur), leaving the wholesale-eligible flower menu at seven strains: Jai Alai, Cakewalk, Night Cap, End Game, Open Mic, Keepsake, and Hotlanta.

BOTANICAL SCIENCES — NO RESPONSE DESPITE OUTREACH

Several outreach attempts to Botanical Sciences this week went unanswered, even after Fine Fettle submitted an order for their wholesale products. Chris's read is that they may be waiting to see GA's 0.5g Vape line before engaging further — worth revisiting once vape test results are back.

AURA 1g DISPOSABLES SOLD OUT AGAIN — BACKUP BATCHES IN THE PRODUCTION PIPELINE

The first two AURA AIO batches out of testing (Galactic Gas 963u, Super Lemon Haze 931u) were delivered entirely to retail in Wk1, leaving 0 available at cultivation before any wholesale allocation could be made. Once we have a clear understanding of demand and strains in rotation, increasing batch size from 900 to 1,500–1,800 units per production run would build enough surplus to support a wholesale allocation alongside retail, and would reduce how often new production/testing cycles are needed. Worth tracking whether the same logic should extend to Vape (510) batch sizing once confirmed.

CAKEWALK JOINS THE WHOLESALE MENU — TWO-BATCH STRUCTURE (WK1)

Cakewalk carries two batches at cultivation (214u active + 1,059u on-deck) and was made available on the wholesale menu alongside Jai Alai in Wk1. The on-deck batch will automatically activate once the first sells through.

0.5g VAPES (510) — STILL IN TESTING, NO CHANGE

Vapes remain sold out at cultivation with no deliveries to either channel this week. Carrying forward the testing timeline flagged at the end of July.
