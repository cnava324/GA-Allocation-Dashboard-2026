TRUE BLISS – DULUTH: NEW LOCATION, FIRST PRE-ORDER (160u / $4,000)

True Bliss – Duluth placed its first order on 10/16 (Fine_F-118): Tucked In 80u and Georgia Pie 80u, $4,000 on Net 14 (due 11/9), delivering Mon 10/26. It is also the first order placed off the expanded three-strain wholesale menu, and it will be October's first wholesale revenue when it lands in the 10/31 column.

PAYMENTS: TRUE BLISS PAID $18,000, EVERY DELIVERED INVOICE IS CURRENT

True Bliss – Buckhead Fine_F-103 ($10,000) and True Bliss – Downtown Fine_F-104 ($8,000) were both paid on 10/16, 9 days after their 10/7 due date and the same day Duluth placed its order. Every delivered GA wholesale invoice is now Fully Paid. Across six invoices, True Bliss has paid anywhere from on time to 14 days late, so a courtesy reminder a few days before Duluth's 11/9 due date is worth scheduling.

INVENTORY UNCHANGED SINCE 10/15; NO NEW BATCHES

Cultivation holds at 8,427u with 1,275u reserved. No new batches were added this week. After the 10/26 deliveries, about 7,152u will remain, of which 5,967u is flower. Tinctures are still sold out, Soft Drops and Concentrates will be sold out after Athens, and Capsules (330u) have not moved since FFD Macon's opening order.

TESTED PIPELINE: ONLY COMFORT CAPSULES AND TWO SAUS END GAME BATCHES

Per Chris (10/21), the only batches that have passed testing but are not yet packaged or on the menu are a Dr. Henry's Comfort Capsules 200mg batch and two SAUS End Game concentrate batches. The End Game batches test in the mid-40% to 50% range, well below the 66%–76% SAUS Badder that sold through this month, so it is undecided whether they will be packaged for sale. Nothing for AIO, Tinctures, Soft Drops, or 0.5g Vapes has cleared testing yet, so after the 10/26 deliveries the next restock in those categories depends on batches still in production or testing.

R&D FLOWER: ONE TO SEVEN CASES LEFT PER STRAIN AFTER ATHENS

After the Athens reservations, the R&D batches are down to Fritterz x Finessin 287u, Jai Alai x Finessin #1 86u, Jai Alai x Finessin #2 44u, and Hulkberry 14u (below a full case). Bright Side has 119u unreserved and Velvet Punch 62u. All stay restricted for the FF stores.
