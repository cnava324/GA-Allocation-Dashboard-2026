SECTION 5 — STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS Launch notes · scope · data caveats

WHOLESALE FLOWER AVAILABILITY MAY HAVE JUMPED FROM 800u TO ~3,370u, PENDING CONFIRMATION

The 10/9 export changes several Restricted flags. Tucked In is now one unrestricted line (1,950u unreserved, 48 cases) instead of an 800u unrestricted line plus a 1,550u restricted line. Georgia Pie's new batch 2 (680u, 17 cases, created 10/5) and OG Kush batch 1 (738u, 18 cases) also show unrestricted, with a second OG Kush batch (419u, 16.42%) on deck and unrestricted. If confirmed, wholesale-available flower is about 3,368u (84 cases) today and 3,787u once the on-deck OG Kush activates. That would give True Bliss and the pharmacy accounts three strains to choose from instead of one.

OCTOBER WK1, RETAIL-ONLY WEEK: 3,135u / $85,504

No wholesale orders or deliveries this week. Retail delivered to all 4 FF stores (Fine_F-110 to 113): Athens 1,269u / $33,828 and Evans 290u / $7,980 on 10/6, Smyrna 852u / $22,752 and Decatur 724u / $20,944 on 10/8. AIO led revenue ($40,500, 47% of the week), followed by Georgia Pie flower ($22,000). Four new batches made their first store allocations: Georgia Pie flower, King Louis XIII AIO, Dr. Henry's Uplift Soft Drops, and Dr. Henry's Sleep Capsules.

PAYMENTS: TRUE BLISS $18,000 NOW PAST DUE, PHARMACY PLACE PAID

True Bliss – Buckhead Fine_F-103 ($10,000) and True Bliss – Downtown Fine_F-104 ($8,000) were due 10/7 on Net 14 and still show Unpaid in APEX. The Pharmacy Place Fine_F-109 ($3,000, COD) has posted as Fully Paid. Every other GA wholesale invoice since July is Fully Paid. True Bliss paid its two August invoices 6 and 1 days late respectively, so a quick follow-up now is consistent with their pattern rather than a red flag.

SIX NEW FLOWER BATCHES THIS WEEK, INCLUDING A 25.78% R&D STRAIN

Six new flower lines added 2,603u: R&D Eazy Peazy 486u at 25.78% THC (the highest-potency flower GA has carried), Velvet Punch 542u (19.42%), OG Kush batch 2 419u (16.42%, on deck), Keepsake batch 2 378u (11%), R&D Jai Alai x Finessin #2 364u (15.21%), and R&D Hulkberry 414u (16.53%, added 10/9). All but OG Kush batch 2 are Restricted for the FF stores. Inventory reconciles exactly: 12,902u (10/2) minus 3,135u delivered plus 2,603u new equals 12,370u.

FFD MACON: RESERVATION GROWS TO 4,048u (+740u)

New reservations on Fine_F-102 this week: Georgia Pie 200u, Mixed Greens +80u (now fully reserved at 280u), King Louis XIII AIO 160u, Dr. Henry's Uplift Soft Drops 100u, Dr. Henry's Comfort Capsules 100u, and Dr. Henry's Sleep Capsules 100u. Still pending final license approval with no delivery date. The $67,876.82 invoice value on file is now well below the order's true size; an updated invoice would let us log the dollar value. Not counted in Section 2 until it delivers.

1G DISPOSABLES (AIO): WHOLESALE WINDOW NARROWS

Retail drew 1,080u of AIO this week, the most since the August restock, including 560u of the new King Louis XIII. After the FFD Macon reservation, only 332u is unreserved (Lemon Cherry Gelato 187u, King Louis XIII 120u, Green Crack 25u), down from 1,572u last week. At this retail pace, a wholesale AIO allocation is unlikely until another batch clears testing. This reinforces the August recommendation to raise AIO batch sizes from about 900u to 1,500–1,800u once strains in rotation are settled.

CAPSULES AND SOFT DROPS: UNRESERVED SUPPLY DRAWN DOWN

Dr. Henry's Comfort Capsules moved for the first time since 9/10 (300u) and the new Sleep Capsules made their first allocations (200u), dropping unreserved Capsules from 1,030u to 330u (Sleep 199u, Comfort 131u). Soft Drops unreserved is down to 97u after the first 400u of Uplift went to stores. If Capsules are the first non-flower category tested on wholesale, 330u is enough for roughly 16 cases at the proposed 20u/case.

0.5G VAPES AND TINCTURES: FULLY RESERVED

The last 14u of unreserved 510 vapes went to FFD Athens. All 320u of vapes and all 264u of Tinctures at cultivation are reserved for FFD Macon. New Tincture batches are in the works from production.

CARRIED FROM SEPTEMBER: MENU EXPANSION, CASE SIZING, PIPELINE

Wholesale menu expansion beyond flower and vapes remains open; the margin hurdle is cleared for Capsules, Soft Drops, and select Tinctures, and the 10 / 20 / 30 case-size proposal is still pending a decision. Professional Pharmacy (Dalton) and the Canton lead remain POS-gated. Tifton is being onboarded to Releaf POS by the end of October, with the GA Map Locator in development to raise comffy brand awareness.
