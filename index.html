CARRIED FROM SEPTEMBER: MENU EXPANSION, CASE SIZING, PIPELINE

Cultivation team confirmed case counts moving forward: Concentrates and 1,200mg Tinctures will be packed at 20 units per case (larger jars), and all other products at 40 units per case. Limited biomass availability will have us holding off from launching categories with favorable wholesale margins like capsules and soft drops, until we have enough distillate and production capabilities to keep a consistent demand for our stores with slight excess to offer for wholesale accounts.

EDIBLE BATCH INCONSISTENCIES: MG VARIANCE WELL OUTSIDE A ±5% STANDARD

Recent gummy, capsule, and tincture batches are testing well off their labeled dose. Most 200mg batches come in 10% to 20% low, and some are much further off: two Dr. Henry's Soft Drops batches are short by 77mg to 88mg (38% to 44%). Our 1,200mg tincture is 127mg short, and one capsule batch tests 28% over label. The newest Comfort Soft Drops batch (10/21) also tests 13% low at 174.81mg. Only a few batches fall within ±5%. Recommendation: set a ±5% release spec and remediate any batch outside it before it ships, so patients get consistent, reliable dosing.
